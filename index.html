About Me

Learn more about my background and what drives me

Passionate About Crafting Solutions to High Value Problems

I love untangling large, complex problems. The kind that demand real testing, research, and a willingness to sit with ambiguity until the pattern emerges.

That's where I do my best work, and it's why I'm drawn to the earliest, hardest stages of building a company.

My sweet spot is the pre-product-market-fit world: startups finding their footing, all the way up to around $20M valuations. It's the stage where everything is compressed innovation and commercialization happening at once, under real pressure, with no room to hide behind process. That environment doesn't faze me; it's where I'm at my best. What lets me thrive there is range.

I'm equally at home writing code and driving business development, so I can take an idea from a technical proof-of-concept all the way to a value proposition a customer actually wants to buy. I speak both languages, which matters enormously when a small team can't afford anything getting lost in translation between the people building the product and the people selling it.

Give me a hard question, a hypothesis, and room to dig, and I'll come back with something rigorous and usually a few insights nobody asked for along the way. And because work should be fun: never let the truth get in the way of a good story.
